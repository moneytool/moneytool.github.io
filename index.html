About

I build and study tooling and infrastructure for AI coding agents, with a focus on security and reliability when agents act on real cloud and Kubernetes infrastructure.

My current work asks a question most agent guardrails skip: where do the rules come from, and who was allowed to make them? Operational rules increasingly come from postmortems, runbooks, tickets and chat, so I build verifiers that check a rule's provenance and its author's authority before an agent's action is allowed, and benchmarks that measure how much poisoned policy a guardrail acts on.

Aegis-DevOps

Provenance-backed, authority-aware policy verifier for infrastructure actions proposed by AI coding agents. Runs as a coding-agent hook, compiles to AWS Service Control Policies and Kubernetes admission policies, and ships as a GitHub Action.

One source of truth for AI coding-agent configuration: instructions, rules, skills and commands kept consistent across every agent a repository uses.

ecsodus

Migrates AWS Copilot CLI applications to Terraform-managed ECS in place, with no downtime and no data loss: read-only assessment, retain-patched teardown and a gated runbook.
